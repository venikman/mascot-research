## 1) Target × Boundary × Scenes/Apps
- **Who to transform (target):** working **system engineers** and adjacent practitioners with **beginner‑level systems thinking**.
- **Boundary it crosses:** from _fragmented, tool‑driven habits_ → _boundary‑aware, role‑clear, evidence‑anchored practice_ **inside an adult‑learning context** (work/life self‑management). Use FPF’s **`U.BoundedContext`** as the “room of meaning” the mascot points to in content cards.
- **Primary scenes / apps:** daily stand‑up, incident bridge, PR/code review, planning board, personal habit tracker, calendar block, whiteboard/Miro, Slack/Teams message, terminal overlay, kitchen counter “habit loop”.
## 2) U.Methods the mascot enacts (slots to plug the memes into)
> Format: **Name** — _Inputs → Key steps → Outputs (MethodDescription artifact the Work instantiates)_. (Role–Method–Work split stays explicit in captions.)
1. **Boundary‑Check in 30s** — _Input:_ draft doodle or task list → _Steps:_ draw boundary ring · mark in/out flows · name context card → _Output:_ **Context Card** (MethodDescription: _U.BoundedContext quick sheet_).
2. **Role–Badge Before Action** — _Input:_ planned step → _Steps:_ pick **required roles** · assign **badge** (RoleAssignment) · check **RSG state = enactable** → _Output:_ **Go/No‑Go stamp** (MethodDescription: _Green‑Gate checklist_).
3. **Plan ≠ Run Split** — _Input:_ SOP/code → _Steps:_ label **MethodDescription** vs **Work** log lanes → _Output:_ **Two‑lane card** (MethodDescription: _Design/Run separator_).
4. **Service vs Capability Triage** — _Input:_ “we provide X” claim → _Steps:_ classify **Service (promise)** vs **Capability (can do)** → _Output:_ **Two‑box verdict card** (MethodDescription: _L‑SERV & capability sheet_).
5. **Evidence Anchor** — _Input:_ result claim → _Steps:_ attach **carrier** link · set **polarity/scope/time** → _Output:_ **Evidence ticket** (MethodDescription: _EvidenceRole binder_).
6. **Bridge or It Didn’t Happen** — _Input:_ cross‑team reuse → _Steps:_ author **Bridge** · record **CL** & losses → _Output:_ **Bridge chip** (MethodDescription: _Alignment template_).
## 3) Media grammar (repeatable system)

### 3.1 Visual DNA (unique to mascot)
- **Codename:** **Maskot‑ρ** (ρ = “role”). Non‑human **capsule** body with a thin **orbit ring** (boundary). Face: two dots + straight mouth (neutral; not cutesy).
- **Core symbols:** **Boundary ring**, **Role badge**, **Green‑Gate light**, **Bridge tile**, **Evidence stamp**.
- **Palette:** Ink #111827, Neutral #9CA3AF, Blueprint #2563EB (info), **Green‑Gate #22C55E**, Amber #F59E0B (warning), Red #EF4444 (blocked). High‑contrast AA.
- **Materials:** flat color with subtle 1‑px inner stroke; soft shadow 8–12 px; no gradients by default.
### 3.2 Pose library (12)
1. **Point‑the‑Boundary** (ring drawn around a scene) — ¾ view, mid‑shot.
2. **Badge‑On** (pins role badge to chest).
3. **Green‑Gate** (holds green card; small halo) — approval.
4. **Blocked** (red cross over gate) — SoD/No‑Go.
5. **Bridge‑Build** (places tile between two labeled cards).
6. **Evidence‑Stamp** (presses stamp onto doc corner).
7. **Plan/Run Split** (hands separate two lanes).
8. **Service≠Capability** (balances two labeled boxes).
9. **Observe** (loupes over meter) — measurement.
10. **Assemble‑Γ** (stacks three blocks) — composition.
11. **Context‑Card Hold** (shows “Room: …” title bar).
12. **Handoff** (passes badge to another figure) — role transfer.
### 3.3 Signature gestures
- **Boundary sweep**, **Badge flip**, **Bridge click‑in**, **Checklist tick**, **Gate light toggle**.
### 3.4 2–3 s motion loops (seamless)
- **Boundary Sweep Loop:** ring draws, locks, subtle glow.
- **Badge Flip Loop:** flips role badge; state LED toggles.
- **Gate Pulse Loop:** red→amber→green idle pulse.
- **Bridge Snap Loop:** tile slides, _click_, alignment line appears.
### 3.5 Camera & framing
- **Shots:** hero medium (waist‑up), OTS to card, top‑down desk.
- **Safe areas:** 7.5% margins; text never <24 px at 1080×1920.
### 3.6 Background style
- **Clean grid** + **sticky‑note cards**; faint blueprint lines; contextual labels: **Context**, **Role**, **MethodDescription**, **Work** tagged pills (never mixed).
### 3.7 Caption templates (language/typography/tone)
- **Language/tone:** Plain EN; crisp, teacherly, zero jargon; 1 idea per card.
- **Templates (max 48 chars/line):**
    - “**Boundary first. Then act.**”
    - “**Badge → Gate → Go.**”
    - “**Service ≠ Capability.**”
    - “**Plan ≠ Run. Keep both.**”
    - “**Bridge it, or don’t mix.**”
    - “**Evidence wears a date.**”
- **Structure:** **H1 (16–24 px)** + **cue line (12–16 px)** + micro‑label chips.
- **Fonts:** Inter (titles/labels), JetBrains Mono (code/chips), fallback system sans/mono.
### 3.8 Voice lines (VO/Speech bubbles; ≤3s read)
- “**Draw the room.**” · “**Wear the right badge.**” · “**Green gate? Proceed.**” · “**Recipe here, run there.**” · “**Promise out, ability in.**”
### 3.9 Audio cues (subtle)
- **Ring draw:** pencil‑scratch + soft _whoosh_.
- **Badge flip:** _clack_.
- **Gate change:** soft tri‑tone (red low, green high).
- **Bridge snap:** _click_ with short reverb.
### 3.10 Aspect ratios & export
- **9:16**, **1:1**, **16:9** masters; 1080‑base; transparent PNG (stills), SVG (icons), MP4/H.264 (loops), GIF (≤3s).
- **Alt text:** “Maskot‑ρ flips a role badge; gate turns green.”
### 3.11 Do / Don’t (guardrails)
- **Do:** keep **Role–Method–Work** labels distinct; always show **Context** on top of cards.
- **Don’t:** anthropomorphize documents; **epistemes don’t act**; no tool vendor logos.
### 3.12 Creator’s playground (variable slots)
- **Badge icon** (role‑specific) · **Card color** (context‑specific) · **Micro‑prop** (scene‑specific) — swap freely while keeping ring/badge/gate constant.
